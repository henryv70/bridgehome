# Bank and wallet marks

These locally bundled brand assets identify institutions in the demo bank directory. The app makes no runtime requests to logo providers.

`sources.json` records each institution, original domain, downloaded asset URL, and SHA-256 of the bundled file. Most marks are the institution’s site icon cached by Google’s favicon service. Navy Federal and M-Pesa use the Wikimedia Commons SVG logos; Equity uses the Wikimedia-derived logo distributed by Trouverlogo.

The Navy Federal SVG is CC0 (Mawillcockson): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Navy_Federal_Credit_Union_Logo.svg
The M-Pesa SVG is a public-domain text/shape logo: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:M-PESA_LOGO-01.svg
Equity source reference: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fichier:Equity_Group_Logo.png

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Their inclusion identifies the demo institution and does not imply a partnership or connection to the bank.
